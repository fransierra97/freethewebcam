# Always

- Only adults who are verified and registered, and who consent at all times.
- Respect in chat: no harassment, threats, hate speech or other people's personal data.

# Never

- Minors, or anyone who looks like a minor, under any circumstances.
- Content without consent, or recorded or shared without the permission of the people in it.
- Sexual violence, incest, animals, drugs, or people who are asleep, unconscious or incapacitated.
- Asking for or accepting in-person meetings in exchange for money.
- Spam, scams or ads for other sites.
