# Illegal or non-consensual content

If you see a minor, content without consent or any other illegal content, report it from the room (“Report this room”) or write to {{email}}. We review it as a priority, cut the stream if needed and notify the authorities where the law requires it.

# Copyright

If you believe something on {{site}} infringes your copyright, send {{email}}:

- Your contact details.
- Which work has been infringed and where the content is (link).
- A good-faith statement that the use is not authorized.
- A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and that you are the owner or act on the owner's behalf.
- Your signature.

The person who posted the content may send a counter-notice with the same details.
