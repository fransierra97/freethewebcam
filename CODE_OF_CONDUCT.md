# Code of conduct

We want a welcoming community for everyone who contributes to FreeTheWebcam.

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, sexualized language or imagery in project spaces, personal attacks or doxxing.
- Respect privacy: never share other people's personal data, credentials or private content.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break these rules and may block repeat offenders.
Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub.
